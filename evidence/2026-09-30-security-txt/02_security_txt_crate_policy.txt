115-
116-> We pay a bug bounty at our discretion after verifying the bug, up to 10% of value at risk, limited by a maximum of $x. This bounty is only paid out if details about the security issues have not been provided to third parties before a fix has been introduced and verified. Furthermore, the reporter is in no way allowed to exploit the issue without our explicit consent.
